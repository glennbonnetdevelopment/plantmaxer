PlantMaxer Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

PlantMaxer does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and the app does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

Your data

Plants, feeds, strains, seeds, photos, and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they also sync through your own private iCloud database, which only you can access. We cannot see it.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to take plant photos and to scan PlantMaxer barcode labels. Photos you import from your library are copied into the app. Nothing leaves your device except through iCloud sync or files you choose to share.

Backups and sharing

Backups, spreadsheets, labels, and reports are created only when you ask, and go wherever you choose to save or share them.

Deleting your data

Delete items in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on this device. Synced data can be removed in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

Children

PlantMaxer is rated 18+ and is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support link on PlantMaxer's App Store page.
